# Research Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines the standardized research process used throughout the Freelance OS project.

The objective is to ensure that every conclusion is evidence-based, reproducible, and aligned with real market conditions.

This methodology applies to all future research, including:

- Freelance platforms
- AI services
- Market trends
- Pricing
- Competition
- Portfolio prioritization
- Client acquisition

---

# Research Workflow

Every research topic follows the same workflow.

```
Research Question
        ↓
Data Collection
        ↓
Data Validation
        ↓
Analysis
        ↓
Conclusion
        ↓
Recommendation
```

Each step must be completed before moving to the next.

---

# Step 1 — Define the Research Question

Every research begins with a single clear question.

Examples:

- Which AI services have the highest demand?
- Which freelance platform is best for beginners?
- What is the average budget for Browser Automation projects?
- Which industries are adopting AI the fastest?

Avoid broad or ambiguous questions.

---

# Step 2 — Collect Data

Data should be collected from multiple independent sources whenever possible.

Priority is given to:

- Official platform reports
- Public datasets
- Industry reports
- Academic research
- Government publications
- Public financial reports

Community discussions may be used only as supporting evidence.

---

# Step 3 — Validate the Data

Before using any information, verify:

- Publication date
- Original source
- Consistency with other sources
- Business relevance

If multiple trustworthy sources disagree, document the disagreement instead of selecting one without explanation.

---

# Step 4 — Analyze

The analysis should answer:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What does it mean?
- Does it affect freelancers?
- Does it change our roadmap?

Analysis should focus on business implications rather than technical curiosity.

---

# Step 5 — Draw Conclusions

Conclusions should be:

- Short
- Objective
- Evidence-based
- Actionable

Avoid speculation.

---

# Step 6 — Make Recommendations

Every research document should end with practical recommendations.

Recommendations should answer:

- What should we do?
- What should we build?
- What should we prioritize?
- What should we avoid?

---

# Documentation Standards

Every research document should follow this structure:

1. Executive Summary
2. Current Situation
3. Data
4. Analysis
5. Conclusions
6. Recommendations

This ensures consistency across the entire repository.

---

# Review Checklist

Before a document is considered complete:

- [ ] Research question is clearly defined
- [ ] Sources are documented
- [ ] Data has been validated
- [ ] Conclusions are supported by evidence
- [ ] Recommendations are actionable
- [ ] No unsupported assumptions are included

---

# Definition of Done

A research document is considered complete only when:

- The conclusions can be traced back to reliable sources.
- Another researcher could reproduce the same process.
- The recommendations directly support the objective of acquiring freelance clients.
