# Scoring System

## Purpose

This document defines the standardized scoring framework used throughout the Freelance OS project.

The objective is to evaluate freelance opportunities using measurable criteria rather than subjective opinions.

Every service, platform, niche, and business opportunity will be scored using the same methodology.

---

# Scoring Framework

The maximum score is **100 points**.

| Criteria | Weight | Description |
|----------|-------:|-------------|
| Market Demand | 30 | Current market demand and growth potential |
| Competition | 20 | Difficulty of winning projects (lower competition = higher score) |
| Average Budget | 15 | Average project value and pricing potential |
| Client ROI | 15 | Expected business value delivered to clients |
| Portfolio Difficulty | 10 | Difficulty of building a convincing portfolio/demo |
| Technical Complexity | 5 | Technical implementation difficulty |
| Retainer Potential | 5 | Opportunity for recurring monthly revenue |

**Maximum Score: 100**

---

# Criteria Definitions

## 1. Market Demand (30)

Measures how frequently clients request this service.

Indicators include:

- Job volume
- Search trends
- Platform demand
- Market growth

Scoring:

| Score | Interpretation |
|-------:|----------------|
| 9–10 | Very High |
| 7–8 | High |
| 5–6 | Medium |
| 3–4 | Low |
| 1–2 | Very Low |

---

## 2. Competition (20)

Measures how difficult it is for a new freelancer to win projects.

Indicators include:

- Number of competing freelancers
- Proposal saturation
- Market maturity

Higher score = lower competition.

---

## 3. Average Budget (15)

Measures average project value.

Example ranges:

| Budget | Score |
|---------|------:|
| > $5,000 | 10 |
| $2,000–5,000 | 8 |
| $1,000–2,000 | 6 |
| $500–1,000 | 4 |
| < $500 | 2 |

---

## 4. Client ROI (15)

Measures how much business value the service creates.

Indicators:

- Time saved
- Revenue increase
- Cost reduction
- Productivity improvement

Services with measurable ROI receive higher scores.

---

## 5. Portfolio Difficulty (10)

Measures how difficult it is to demonstrate the service.

Higher scores indicate that a freelancer can build a convincing demo quickly without requiring enterprise infrastructure.

---

## 6. Technical Complexity (5)

Measures implementation difficulty.

Higher scores indicate simpler implementation.

This criterion exists because easier services allow faster portfolio development and client delivery.

---

## 7. Retainer Potential (5)

Measures long-term revenue opportunities.

Indicators:

- Maintenance
- Monitoring
- Workflow improvements
- Feature expansion
- AI optimization

---

# Final Score Formula

```
Final Score =
Demand +
Competition +
Budget +
ROI +
Portfolio +
Complexity +
Retainer
```

Maximum Score = **100**

# Opportunity Classification

| Score | Classification |
|-------:|----------------|
| 90–100 | Tier 1 — Highest Priority |
| 80–89 | Tier 2 — High Opportunity |
| 70–79 | Tier 3 — Good Opportunity |
| 60–69 | Tier 4 — Niche Opportunity |
| <60 | Low Priority |

---

# Scoring Rules

Before assigning a score:

- Collect current market data.
- Validate the data.
- Compare multiple sources.
- Apply the scoring criteria consistently.
- Document any assumptions.

Scores should be reviewed periodically as market conditions change.

---

# Definition of Done

A service or platform is considered evaluated only after:

- Reliable data has been collected.
- Every criterion has been scored.
- The final score has been calculated.
- The reasoning behind the score is documented.
