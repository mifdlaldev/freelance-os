# Follow-Up System

## Golden Rules
- **80% penjualan terjadi setelah follow-up ke-5**
- **Follow-up bukan nge spam — ini nurturing**
- **Setiap follow-up harus kasih value baru**

## Sequence Template
| # | Timing | Message |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Initial proposal/outreach |
| 2 | Day 3 | Follow-up: tambah 1 insight |
| 3 | Day 7 | Share relevant case study |
| 4 | Day 14 | "Still interested?" + offer |
| 5 | Day 30 | Soft check-in, share useful resource |

## Tools
- HubSpot CRM (free) — Track follow-up
- Boomerang (Gmail) — Schedule follow-up
- Notion — Personal CRM

## Tips
- Catat detail personal di CRM untuk personalisasi
- Follow-up via LinkedIn + email (multi-channel)
- Stop setelah 5x tanpa respons — move on
- Re-engage leads lama tiap 3 bulan
