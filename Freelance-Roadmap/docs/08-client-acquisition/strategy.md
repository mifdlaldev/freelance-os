# Acquisition Strategy

## Channel Matrix
| Channel | Effort | Conversion Rate | Client Quality |
|---------|--------|----------------|---------------|
| Upwork Bidding | Tinggi | 10–20% | Medium |
| LinkedIn Outreach | Tinggi | 5–15% | High |
| Referral | Rendah | 50%+ | High |
| Content Marketing | Tinggi (awal) | 2–5% | High |
| Cold Email | Sedang | 1–5% | Medium |
| Komunitas | Rendah | 10–20% | Medium |

## Strategy Mix
**Fase 1 (0–3 bulan)**: Upwork + Komunitas (fast wins)
**Fase 2 (3–6 bulan)**: Upwork + LinkedIn + Referral
**Fase 3 (6–12 bulan)**: LinkedIn + Referral + Content Marketing
