# AI Freelance Market Research (July–December 2026)

## Purpose

This section contains data-driven research about the global AI freelance market from July to December 2026.

The objective is to identify the highest-probability services for acquiring freelance clients, based on measurable market demand rather than assumptions.

---

## Scope

This research covers:

- Freelance platforms
- AI automation trends
- AI agent trends
- AI workflow trends
- Business demand
- Pricing benchmarks
- Competition analysis
- Service opportunities

---

## Primary Data Sources

The research prioritizes information from:

- Upwork
- Fiverr
- Contra
- LinkedIn Jobs
- PeoplePerHour
- Guru
- Wellfound
- Reddit
- Official market reports
- Public funding reports
- AI company reports

---

## Research Principles

Every conclusion inside this repository must follow these rules:

1. Evidence before opinion.
2. Multiple sources whenever possible.
3. Recent data is prioritized.
4. Business ROI is more important than technical complexity.
5. Services are ranked by client acquisition probability.

This folder contains:

| File | Description |

## Goal

By the end of this research we should know:

- Which services sell the easiest
- Which services pay the highest
- Which niches have the lowest competition
- Which platforms should receive the most effort
- What portfolio projects should be built first

This research becomes the foundation for every roadmap, portfolio decision, pricing strategy, and client acquisition plan inside this repository.
