# Cold Email Template

## Subject Line Examples
- "Quick question about [Company]'s [specific thing]"
- "Idea for [Company]'s [problem]"
- "[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out"

## Email Template
```
Subject: Helping [Company] with [specific goal]

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Company] and noticed [specific observation/insight].

I specialize in [service] and recently helped [similar client] achieve [result with metric].

I have a few ideas on how [Company] could [benefit]. Would you be open to a quick 15-min call?

Here's my portfolio: [link]

Best,
[Name]
```

## Follow-up Sequence
1. **Day 0** — Initial email
2. **Day 3** — "Just bumping this up. Also wanted to share [relevant resource]."
3. **Day 7** — "Still interested? Happy to hop on a quick call at your convenience."
4. **Day 14** — "Last follow-up. Feel free to save my contact for future needs."
5. **Day 30** — Check-in with new case study / insight

- Apollo.io — Prospecting
- Lemlist / Instantly — Cold email campaigns
