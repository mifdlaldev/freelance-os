# Contra Proposal Strategy

## Overview
Contra berbeda — fokus ke portfolio dan direct booking, bukan bidding.

## Strategi
1. **Profile is your proposal** — Lengkapi profile dengan case studies
2. **Direct booking** — Aktifkan fitur booking
3. **Contra Discover** — Biar klien nemuin kamu
4. **Apply to jobs** — Kirim proposal untuk job posting

## Proposal Contra
```
Hi! I'm interested in helping with your project.

I have experience in [skill] and recently completed [project] with [result].

I can start [timeline] and deliver within [X] days/weeks.

Here's my portfolio: [link]

Let's chat!
```

## Tips
- 0% komisi — kamu dapat full payment
- Fokus ke profile & portfolio quality
- Responsif karena Contra kurasi klien
